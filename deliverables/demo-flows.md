# Demo-video-friendly Foundation flows

These are concise walkthroughs for a Foundation demonstration. Keep network
access disabled to show the offline experience. Do not present demo-only values
as verified personal results or production emission factors.

## 1. Guest estimate

1. Launch the app and leave the account flow unused.
2. Select the clearly marked demo region.
3. Enter example activity inputs; identify the result as demo-only.
4. Open each comparison detail and point out source, date, methodology, and
   accounting boundary. Explain that incompatible references are contextual
   and are not displayed as comparison rings.
5. Continue to Home without registering.

## 2. Home destinations

1. Show the Home screen and open each of the 11 module destinations.
2. Return Home between destinations; explain that these are Foundation
   destinations and detailed module workflows are deferred.
3. Show About Us from Home and locate Gallery and Contact Us in secondary
   navigation.

## 3. Regional selection

1. Open Settings and display the selected region independently from language.
2. Select Pakistan and show the cited electricity factor and PKR currency.
3. Point out that transport and recipe impact values are unavailable because
   no cited factors are included.
4. State that the IGES v11.6 grid-factor reconciliation remains a release gate.

## 4. Privacy controls

1. Open the offline privacy policy and summarize local-only account storage.
2. Show the export action; explain that credential verifiers are excluded.
3. Open and cancel deletion to demonstrate the confirmation step.
4. Explain that confirmed deletion cannot be undone and cannot guarantee
   forensic erasure or remove user-created exports.
