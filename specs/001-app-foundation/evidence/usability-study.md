# Guest estimate usability study

**Status**: Pending manual study with real participants. No participant results
have been collected or inferred.

## Study objective and success criterion

Measure whether a first-time user can complete and view the guest estimate,
including all three comparison references, within 60 seconds without assistance.
The acceptance criterion is at least 9 of 10 participants meeting the task.

## Moderator preparation

- Recruit 10 participants who have not used this app.
- Use the same supported device setup for each participant and reset the app to
  first launch before each session.
- Record participant code (P01–P10), start/end time, completion outcome, and
  whether assistance was required. Do not record names or unrelated personal
  information.
- Ask for permission before recording the screen. Screen recording is optional.
- Use the app's clearly marked demo region and synthetic inputs only.

## Read-aloud script

> You are about to try an early version of a sustainable-living app. Please
> explore it as you normally would. Starting from the first screen, get to the
> guest footprint estimate and review the comparison information. You may stop
> at any time. I will not guide you unless you ask to stop.

Start the timer when the first screen is visible. Stop it when the participant
has viewed the estimate result and each of the three labeled reference cards.
Do not coach, interpret the numbers, or count time spent asking for help as
unassisted completion.
