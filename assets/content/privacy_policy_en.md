## Your data stays on this device

Sustainable Living Guide works offline. Optional account details, settings, and saved estimates are stored in the app's local database on this device. The app does not send this information to a server.

## Optional accounts

Creating an account is optional. Public browsing is available without registration. The app stores a normalized login identifier and an Argon2id password verifier; it does not store your password in readable form.

## Device security

Keep your device and its screen lock secure. Anyone with access to an unlocked device may be able to use data stored by this app. Removing the app or clearing its storage may permanently remove its local data.

## Export and deletion

You can request an export of the app data included in its documented export format. Credential verifiers and secret keys are excluded. You can also choose to delete local app data; the app asks for confirmation before deletion. Deletion removes the app's local data from this device and cannot be undone. It cannot guarantee forensic erasure from flash storage. You are responsible for deleting any copies you exported.
